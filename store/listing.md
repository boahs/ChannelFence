## Summary

Block YouTube channels and creators across Home, search, recommendations, Shorts, comments, and direct visits.

## Detailed description

ChannelFence is an open-source, privacy-first YouTube channel blocker for Chrome. Block a YouTube channel or creator once, and ChannelFence hides their content across supported Home feeds, search results, recommendations, Shorts, playlists, comments, and direct pages.

Features:

- Block creators with one click beside their channel name.
- Block directly from a video’s three-dot menu with a clearly labeled ChannelFence action.
- Add a creator manually by pasting their YouTube handle or channel URL.
- Hide videos, Shorts, recommendations, and comments from blocked creators.
- Stop direct visits with a clear blocked-page screen.
- Undo a block immediately or manage your complete block list at any time.
- Pause blocking without deleting your list.
- Import or export a private block-list backup.
- No account, analytics, tracking, advertising, or external server.

Privacy by design:

ChannelFence processes public creator names, channel identifiers, and channel URLs only to provide its blocking feature. Your settings and block list remain in Chrome’s local extension storage and are not transmitted to the developer or third parties.

## Permission justification

### storage

Required to save the user’s enabled state, comment preference, and blocked-creator list locally on the device.

### www.youtube.com and m.youtube.com site access

Required to read public creator identifiers from the page, add user-facing block buttons, hide matching content, and display the direct-navigation block screen. The extension does not request access to other websites.

## Privacy practices answers

- **Does the extension collect or use user data?** Yes, conservatively disclose local processing of website content and user-generated block-list selections.
- **Website content:** Public creator display names, public channel handles/identifiers, and public channel URLs are processed to provide blocking. User-selected records are stored locally.
- **Web history:** Not collected or recorded. The extension does not maintain a list of pages or videos visited.
- **Authentication, personal communications, location, financial, health, and personally identifiable information:** Not collected.
- **Data sale:** No.
- **Data use for unrelated purposes:** No.
- **Data use for creditworthiness or lending:** No.
- **Remote transmission:** None.
- **Limited Use certification:** Yes. Data use is limited to the extension’s disclosed single purpose.

These answers must match the dashboard wording visible at submission time. If Google changes the questionnaire, answer conservatively and keep the dashboard, listing, and privacy policy consistent.
