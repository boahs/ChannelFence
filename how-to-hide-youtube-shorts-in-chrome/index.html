How to hide YouTube Shorts in Chrome

ChannelFence can remove supported Shorts shelves and cards from Home, search, and other feeds without removing the Shorts tab or preventing you from opening the Shorts viewer.

Updated November 8, 2026 · For ChannelFence 0.2.9. These steps use the English labels.

Using desktop Firefox? Install ChannelFence from Firefox Add-ons and open it from Firefox's extensions menu. The Shorts controls below work there too.

Hide Shorts from YouTube feeds

  1. Install ChannelFence from the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Refresh YouTube if it was already open.
  3. Open Chrome's Extensions menu and select ChannelFence. You can pin it for easier access.
  4. Make sure the switch at the top of the popup is on. If the status says Paused, turn that switch back on.
  5. Turn on Hide Shorts in feeds.

This option is off by default and does not need any blocked creators to work. It is also available by choosing Manage block list at the bottom of the popup and finding Hide Shorts in feeds under Settings. Changes are saved automatically and appear in both places for the same browser profile.

The Shorts tab remains available

This option is meant to make regular feeds less intrusive. It does not remove YouTube's Shorts navigation item or disable the direct Shorts viewer.

It does not skip sponsored Shorts playing in the viewer or remove ads inside the video player. Hide sponsored cards on Home is a separate option for sponsored cards in the Home feed.

Block one Shorts creator instead

Creator blocking and hiding Shorts shelves are separate choices. To keep Shorts in your feeds but remove a specific creator, leave Hide Shorts in feeds off and use one of these methods:

  • Use ChannelFence's control on a supported compact Shorts card, or choose ChannelFence: Block creator from its supported three-dot menu, without opening the Short.
  • Use the ChannelFence button in the Shorts viewer to block the current creator and continue to the next Short.
  • Choose Manage block list in the popup, enter the creator's handle or channel URL under Block by handle or channel URL, and select Block creator.

You can also keep creator blocking active while hiding Shorts shelves. Opening the Shorts viewer directly still applies your creator block list.

Turn Shorts back on

Open the popup or settings and turn off Hide Shorts in feeds. Your creator block list stays active, so Shorts from blocked creators can remain hidden.

To unblock a creator, select Unblock in the popup's Recently blocked list or find them through Manage block list. To pause all ChannelFence filtering, use the switch at the top of the popup. Pausing keeps your list and settings, including your Shorts preference, ready for when you enable it again.

If a Shorts shelf still appears

Check that ChannelFence is enabled and Hide Shorts in feeds is on, then refresh the YouTube tab after installing or updating. The option targets supported feed shelves and cards; being able to open the Shorts viewer is expected. If a shelf in a regular feed still slips through, report it with the page URL, a screenshot, and your YouTube language.
