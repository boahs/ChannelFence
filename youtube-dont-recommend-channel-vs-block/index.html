YouTube controls explained

"Don't recommend channel" is not the same as blocking a creator

YouTube describes "Don't recommend channel" as feedback that may tune recommendations. ChannelFence maintains a creator list and hides matches across supported YouTube surfaces.

What "Don't recommend channel" does

YouTube's official help documentation groups "Don't recommend channel" with recommendation controls such as "Not interested." It says this feedback may be used to tune your recommendations and exposes the option on certain pages, including Home and Watch Next.

That makes it useful when you want to influence what YouTube recommends. YouTube also lets you clear this recommendation feedback through Google My Activity.

What a ChannelFence block does

ChannelFence creates an explicit block-list entry for the selected creator. While the extension is enabled, it hides matching content across supported Home feeds, search results, Watch Next recommendations, Shorts, playlists, comments, and direct visits.

You can inspect the list, remove individual creators, pause filtering without deleting the list, and import or export a backup.

These controls can be used for different reasons

Use YouTube's control when you want to give its recommendation system feedback. Use ChannelFence when you want a creator on an explicit, persistent list. ChannelFence does not claim to retrain or replace YouTube's recommendation system.
