Feature comparison

ChannelFence vs. BlockTube

ChannelFence is a focused persistent creator block list. BlockTube is a broader YouTube filtering toolkit with keyword, regular expression, runtime, and advanced JavaScript rules.

Last reviewed August 29, 2026. Features can change after this date.

FeatureChannelFence 0.2.1BlockTube 0.4.8

Choose ChannelFence if...

  • You mainly want to name a creator once and keep that creator on a visible block list.
  • You prefer controls that appear directly beside supported creator names, video menus, and the Shorts viewer.
  • You want a small settings surface without keyword, regular expression, or custom-code rules.

Choose BlockTube if...

  • You need to filter video titles, runtime, keywords, video IDs, or comments by content.
  • You want regular expressions or custom JavaScript-based blocking rules.
  • You need the project's documented Firefox version.
Neither choice is universally better

BlockTube has a wider rule set. ChannelFence deliberately focuses on a persistent creator list and direct YouTube controls. The better fit depends on whether you want a focused workflow or advanced filtering.
